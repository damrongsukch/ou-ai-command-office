# Ou AI Command Office - Canva Brief

## Goal
Create a polished Canva visual for Ou AI Command Office that can be used as a poster, presentation cover, team guide, or dashboard concept reference.

## Visual Direction
- Futuristic executive AI office
- Dark navy command center background
- Clean neon accents: cyan, green, blue, gold, purple
- Friendly chibi agent avatars
- Clear room grouping, not cluttered
- Professional enough for a product/spec presentation

## Main Title
Ou AI Command Office

Personal AI Agent Office for Sales, Portfolio, Documents, Life, and Memory
