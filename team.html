WorkflowNova receives Ou's request, classifies and prioritizes it, dispatches to the right sub agents, consolidates outputs, performs a quality check, stores approved files to Google Drive, writes a log entry, and sends the final output back to Ou.
All sub-agent outputs return to Nova for review before storage and delivery.
