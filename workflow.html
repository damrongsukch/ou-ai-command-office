Operating Rules

Nova first: all work enters through Nova Chief before any specialist is selected.
Relevant agents only: do not wake every agent for simple tasks.
Private data guard: real customer, family, and portfolio data stays in Google Drive or private context.
Token reduction: load only the current route, source manifest, and task-specific prompt.
Ou approval: important outputs remain draft until Ou approves real use.
